About Our Ministry

Grace of God Ministries Pakistan is a faith-based Christian ministry dedicated to spreading the Gospel of Jesus Christ across Pakistan and beyond. Founded on the love of God and the truth of His Word, we reach communities through Bible distribution, prayer outreach, Christian education, and compassionate humanitarian work and orphan families.

Our mission is to bring people closer to Jesus Christ through the Word of God, prayer, evangelism, and practical help for needy communities — particularly the poor, widows, and orphan families who need both spiritual and material support.

We believe that every soul in Pakistan deserves to hear the Good News of Jesus Christ and experience His transforming grace and love.

What We Do

Our Ministry Projects

Through these projects we actively share the love of Christ and meet the spiritual and physical needs of communities across Pakistan.

📖

Free Bible Distribution

Providing the Holy Bible free of charge to individuals, families, and communities who have never had access to God's Word in their own language.

🎁

Christmas Gifts Distribution

Bringing joy to poor and orphan families during the Christmas season through the distribution of gifts, food packages, and clothing.

📅

Monthly Prayer Calendars

Distributing monthly prayer calendars to encourage believers to maintain a daily prayer life and stay connected with God's Word.

📚

Christian Literature Distribution

Sharing Christian books, tracts, devotionals, and literature to strengthen believers and introduce seekers to the Gospel of Jesus Christ.

💻

Online Bible Seminars

Hosting online Bible seminars and training sessions to equip believers with deeper knowledge of Scripture and strengthen their faith.

🏫

Sunday School Ministry

Teaching children the Word of God through Sunday School programs designed to nurture a love for Jesus Christ from an early age.

✝️

Youth Bible Classes

Empowering the next generation through Bible classes, discipleship, and Christ-centered mentorship to equip young leaders for God's Kingdom.

🍞

Food Distribution Program

Providing food packages and essential supplies to poor families and communities, showing the love of Christ through practical compassion.

🤝

Helping Poor & Orphan Families

Supporting orphans, widows, and vulnerable families with financial assistance, educational support, and spiritual care in the name of Jesus.

📢

Evangelism & Outreach Programs

Conducting evangelism crusades, street outreach, and community programs to share the Gospel of Jesus Christ with unreached people.
